Why Did I Pursue Computer Science?

My path into software has been a calculated journey of merging multiple interests in a cohesive and modular manner. My interest in programming was piqued early on as I explored automating complex spreadsheets during my chemistry studies. In the ensuing years, that interest grew into a passion as I explored programming and system architecture in my spare time. During this time, I pursued another interest, physiotherapy, as I also knew it could provide the stability and flexibility to concurrently pursue a software career in the future.

This journey has provided me with two main things:

  1. insight that my ideal work week is full-time software work with flexible part-time physiotherapy work,
  2. a unique perspective and skillset that I am excited to apply within future software roles!
